Tickets and accessMADRING app, QR codes, wristbands

Your ticket is digital and must be accessed through the official MADRING app. Before travelling:

  1. Download the app.
  2. Sign in using the same email address associated with your Official Hospitality account.
  3. Download your tickets in advance. If they don't appear immediately, they'll be available shortly.
  4. Present the live, dynamic QR code in the app at the entrance.
Printed tickets and screenshots aren't accepted. Mobile coverage around the circuit may be limited, so download your tickets before arrival.
Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store

Wristbands can be collected in advance September 4–10, 12:00–21:00, at El Corte Inglés (Castellana, Princesa, Serrano or Preciados). If you can't collect yours beforehand, it's also available at the circuit entrances. Where possible, collect your own wristband in advance for the smoothest arrival.

Where to meet MongoDB The MongoDB team will meet you at the MongoDB table inside the Ignition Club, not at the entrance.

By registering, the person completing registration confirms they've obtained all internal authorizations, corporate approvals and compliance clearances required by their company to accept the invitation and attend.

Circuit map & MongoDB locationAccess E, Gate E3

The Ignition Club is in the MADRING North area. Follow signs to Access E and Gate E3 — MATCH Hospitality staff will direct you the rest of the way.

Where to find the MongoDB hosts Our team will be at the reserved MongoDB table inside the Ignition Club. Once inside, follow the hospitality signage or ask MATCH Hospitality staff to point you there.
Re-entry isn't permitted A valid ticket is required for entry, and ID or proof of age may be requested. Once your ticket is scanned, you can't leave and come back.

MADRING has two independent areas — MADRING North (our access area) and MADRING South — with no connection between them inside the circuit. Check the official map in advance and follow the access point shown on your ticket.

Getting to the circuitTaxi/VTC, private car & parking

Taxi / VTC — recommended

Use the designated taxi and VTC drop-off area — the most convenient and flexible option. MATCH Hospitality staff will direct you from there to the Ignition Club and our MongoDB table.

VTC point
Avenida José Antonio Corrales, between Juan Antonio Samaranch and Manuel Fraga Iribarne (MADRING North)
Tell your driver
MADRING — taxi/VTC area, Avda. del Partenón, 5, 28042 Madrid

From the North VTC point, follow signs to Access E and Gate E3, and allow extra time for the walk in.

Private car

Private vehicles can't access the circuit directly. If you'd rather drive, reserve a parking space in advance at the Riyadh Air Metropolitano Stadium, connected to MADRING by shuttle. Spaces are limited and admission is charged per vehicle.

Please send your vehicle information directly to Maria Alcázar — see Contact.

Entradas y accesoApp MADRING, código QR, pulseras

Su entrada es digital y deberá acceder a ella a través de la aplicación oficial MADRING. Antes de viajar:

  1. Descargue la aplicación.
  2. Inicie sesión con el correo asociado a su cuenta de Official Hospitality.
  3. Descargue sus entradas con antelación. Si no aparecen de inmediato, estarán disponibles en breve.
  4. Muestre en la entrada el código QR dinámico directamente desde la aplicación.
No se aceptan entradas impresas ni capturas de pantalla. La cobertura móvil cerca del circuito puede ser limitada: descargue sus entradas antes de llegar.
Descargar en Google Play Descargar en App Store

Las pulseras se pueden recoger del 4 al 10 de septiembre, de 12:00 a 21:00, en El Corte Inglés (Castellana, Princesa, Serrano o Preciados). Si no puede recogerla con antelación, también estará disponible en las entradas del circuito.

Dónde encontrar a MongoDB El equipo de MongoDB le esperará en la mesa reservada dentro del Ignition Club, no en la entrada.
